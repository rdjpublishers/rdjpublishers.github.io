Free Online Tools & Apps by RDJ Publishers

RDJ Publishers is a growing collection of free web-based tools, browser games, and productivity utilities designed for students and everyday users. Every tool works instantly in your browser — no download, no login, and no charge. Ever.

What Can You Find Here?

Browse tools across five categories: Tools for everyday calculations, Games you can play instantly, Utilities that make daily tasks easier, Fun interactive experiences, and Productivity apps to keep you organised. New projects are added regularly.

Why RDJ Publishers?

All tools are built with simplicity in mind — they load fast, work on mobile, and require no technical knowledge. Whether you're a student tracking attendance, a casual gamer, or someone who needs a quick utility, RDJ Publishers has something for you.

Who Is Behind RDJ Publishers?

RDJ Publishers is a small, independent web studio based in Tamil Nadu, India. We build practical utilities because we use them ourselves — every tool on this site started as a solution to a real problem we (or our friends) ran into. There is no big team, no investors, and no algorithm designing the roadmap. Just a focus on making genuinely useful things that load in one tap.

How Our Tools Work

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. That means your files, images, and calculations never leave your device — there is no server uploading your photos, no account creation, and no data harvesting. Open the page, use the tool, close the tab. Your work stays on your computer.

Built For Students, Useful For Everyone

Our primary audience is students — the people who need a quick attendance calculator, a way to merge PDFs for a project, a colour picker for a design assignment, or a grammar checker before submitting an essay. But the tools are designed to be useful for anyone: small business owners, casual creators, teachers, and people who just want a fast browser-based utility without the bloat of a full software install.

A Note On Privacy

We don't ask for your email, your phone number, or your location. We don't require you to log in. The only data we collect is anonymous usage statistics (via Google Analytics) so we can see which tools people find useful — and standard cookies to keep the site running smoothly. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

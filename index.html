Free Online Tools, Games & Utilities — By RDJ Publishers

RDJ Publishers is a hand-built collection of browser-based tools, games, and utilities that load in one tap and stay out of your way. No downloads, no installs, no account creation, and no surprise paywall at the bottom of the screen. Whether you have five seconds or five hours, every page here is designed to do a single job, do it well, and let you move on with your day.

What Kind Of Tools Will You Find?

Every project on the site falls into one of four categories — and I add new ones regularly:

  • Tools — an attendance calculator, a calculator suite (age, date difference, EMI, GST, income tax, CGPA and more), a PDF toolkit, a QR scanner and generator, ChromaCut (a solid-colour background remover), an image splitter, a colour picker, a grammar checker, a 3D model viewer, Grid Cam (a photo-mosaic maker), a Low Poly 3D model generator, and a prompt gallery.
  • Games — lightweight browser games, a two-player chess game, a chess position editor, and a daily 5-letter word game, that open with a single tap and don't ask for an account.
  • Fun — playful experiments like Touch to Music, an instrument playground with 20+ instrument sounds, built just to make you smile or spark an idea.
  • Productivity — the RDJ Publishers blog, with articles on tech trends, web technologies and online tools.

What Makes These Tools Different?

Most "free tool" sites are bloated, ad-saturated, or quietly harvest your uploads. I took the opposite route. My pages load in under two seconds on a slow 4G connection, the layout works on a six-inch phone screen, and for most tools the actual work — converting, editing, calculating — happens locally in your browser. That means there's no queue, no upload wait, and no privacy tax for using the tool in the first place.

About Me — Who Is Behind RDJ Publishers?

RDJ Publishers is an independent, one-person web studio run by me, Ratheesh, a developer and B.Tech student from Trichy, Tamil Nadu, India. There's no board, no funding round, and no growth team deciding what to build next — just me, building the tools I kept wishing I had, and then rebuilding them slightly better the next weekend. You can read more on my creator page.

The first tool I ever shipped was a tiny attendance calculator I used in college. After that, the catalogue mostly grew the same way: friends texting me "hey, can you also build…?" at odd hours, classmates asking for one more converter before an assignment was due, and a couple of cousins who wanted a chess board that worked on their phone without an account. Today the site carries a mix of those requests and a handful of my own experiments — and the line between the two is honestly blurry.

I'm a one-person studio, which means you'll hear back if you write in, you'll see bugs fixed within a few days, and you'll get the same tool you saw last week the next time you load the page. I don't pretend to be building "the next big platform." What I want is simple: a corner of the internet where you can get a small thing done without signing up, watching a 30-second ad, or handing over your inbox.

How The Tools Actually Work

The short version: most tools run entirely in your browser, not on my servers. When you drop a photo into an editor or paste numbers into a calculator, your input is processed by JavaScript running on your own device, and I never receive your file. There is one exception: the Grammar Checker sends the text you submit to the LanguageTool service to be checked, and says so on its own page. Open the page, use the tool, close the tab. If you want to keep your work, hit the download button; if you don't, it disappears with the tab.

Who Are These Tools For?

Students writing assignments at 2 AM. Teachers prepping handouts on a train. Small business owners who don't want to pay $30 a month for an image converter. Anyone, really, who appreciates opening a link and getting straight to the tool. Most of what I build came out of messages visitors sent me, so if you're a regular here — thank you, and keep the suggestions coming.

A Quick Word On Privacy

I don't ask for your email. I don't ask for your phone number. I don't track which specific files you open. I use Google Analytics to see anonymous, aggregated traffic data so I know which tools are actually getting used, and Google AdSense to show ads; both use cookies, and visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. The full breakdown, including how to opt out, lives in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything to use these tools?

No. Every tool opens in your browser — on desktop or mobile. You don't need an app, an extension, or an admin password. Just open the page and start using the tool.

Can I use RDJ Publishers tools on my phone?

Yes — every page is built mobile-first. The layout adapts to phone screens, tablets, and laptops, and touch gestures work the way you'd expect. Each tool is tested on a mid-range Android phone over mobile data before it goes live.

Will my files, photos, or PDFs get uploaded to a server?

For most tools, no. The work happens locally in your browser, so your images and PDFs stay on your device. The one exception is the Grammar Checker, which sends the text you submit to the LanguageTool service; it says so on its page.

Are there any usage limits or daily caps?

None that you'll hit by accident. No daily caps, no token limits, and no "watch an ad to continue" gates. If a tool does have a constraint, it's listed right at the top of the page.

Can I suggest a new tool idea?

Please do — roughly a third of what is on this site started as a user suggestion. Drop me a note via the Contact page with what you need and what you wish existed. I read every message.

How do I report a bug or something that isn't working?

Use the Contact page. Include the tool name, what you were trying to do, and (if possible) the browser and device. I usually ship a fix within a few days.
